## How it is wired

* All display goes through `lib/discovery/` (`DiscoveryCoordinator.request`); `lib/discovery/ui/` adds
  the presenter (`CoachmarkPresenter`, one card, overlay next to the real control, or inline in the
  font panel) and `DiscoveryHintHost`, which reports facts and keeps the screen calm.
* Calm = no scrolling (and 1.2 s after), no keyboard, route settled and on top (no menu/sheet), 1.5 s on
  view, anchor fully on screen. Max one hint per visit (engine rule); the font hint only near the top of
  the text so that `hours.next_hour` can win at the end.
* Using the real control (tap inside the anchor) counts as learned (`acted`); «Зразумела»/«Не паказваць»
  persist in `discovery_v1` and are not repeated after restart.
* Wrapped at the router: `HoursHintHost` (Ora), `BibleHintHost` (/bible), `PrayersHintHost` (/prayers).
  The panel `_TextScalePanel` gets `PsalmLayoutHintHost` + `HintInlineSlot` when `showPsalmody`.
  Readings/Ordo screens (Stage 3) are not touched.
* Hive: `main.dart` opens the `discovery_v1` box (own box); where it is not open (previews, other
  widget tests) hosts stay silent.
* QA profile (debug/profile only, compile-time `!kReleaseMode`): `--dart-define=DISCOVERY_QA=true`
  removes gaps/quotas; a file `DISCOVERY_QA_RESET` in the app Documents folder (next to `discovery_v1.hive`) forgets hint history at the next launch (the
  `discovery_v1` box only). Settings, favourites, prayers, caches are never touched.

## Known limits

* The font and search capsules sit over the page header / title (not prayer or Scripture text); the Bible
  capsule sits in the free space after the chapter. A small hint over a header is the cost of anchoring
  to a top-right control without a pointer arrow.
* Cyrillic via the simulator keyboard injection came out garbled; the search result was checked with
  the Latin query «Ave».
